## What is an Apostille for Academic Transcripts?

An apostille is a special certificate issued by a government authority. It verifies the authenticity of a signature on a document. This verification allows the document to be recognized in foreign countries that are members of the Hague Apostille Convention. Think of it as an international stamp of approval.

For academic transcripts, the apostille confirms the signature of your university's registrar or other official. It certifies that the person who signed your transcript has the authority to do so. This eliminates the need for further legalization by the destination country's embassy or consulate. This saves you significant time and effort. I help navigate these requirements every day.

## The Apostille Process for Academic Transcripts in California

The process for obtaining an apostille for academic transcripts in California involves specific steps:

1.  **Obtain Official Transcripts:** You must get official transcripts directly from your university or college. The transcripts must include the original signature of the registrar or another authorized school official. The California Secretary of State will not apostille photocopies unless they have been re-certified by the issuing institution.
2.  **Notarization of Registrar's Signature (If Applicable):** In some cases, the foreign country or institution requires the registrar's signature on the transcript to be notarized. If your school allows for this, I can perform this notarization. This step confirms the authenticity of the registrar's signature. Remember, a notary cannot certify the contents or authenticity of the transcript itself, only the signature of the official who signed it.
3.  **Submission to the California Secretary of State:** Once your official transcripts are ready, they must be submitted to the California Secretary of State's office for the apostille. This is where the official apostille certificate is attached.

The California Secretary of State charges $20 per document. Processing in person is same-day. By mail, it is typically 5 to 10 business days. ## What Documents Do You Need for an Academic Transcript Apostille?

To get an apostille for your academic transcripts, you will generally need:
