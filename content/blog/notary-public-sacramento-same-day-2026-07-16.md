## What Identification Do You Need for a Same-Day Notarization?

Proper identification is a critical part of any notarization. For your same-day appointment, you must present a valid, unexpired government issued photo identification. This ID helps me confirm your identity and prevents fraud.

Common forms of acceptable identification include:
*   A California driver's license or state ID card.
*   A US passport or passport card.
*   An inmate identification card issued by the California Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation.
*   A US military ID card.

The ID must include your photograph, signature, and a physical description. Make sure your ID is current and not expired. I cannot proceed with the notarization if your identification is not valid.
