## What is an apostille and why is turnaround time important?

An apostille is essentially an international stamp of approval. It ensures that a public document issued in one country is recognized as legitimate in another country that is a signatory to the 1961 Hague Apostille Convention. Before becoming a California notary in 2014, I practiced law in Peru. I understand the critical need for proper document authentication in international matters.

Common documents requiring an apostille include birth certificates, marriage licenses, academic transcripts, powers of attorney, and corporate documents. If you are adopting a child from another country, getting married abroad, or doing business internationally, you will likely need an apostille. The turnaround time becomes crucial when you have deadlines for immigration applications, university admissions, or business deals. A delay can mean missed opportunities or even legal complications.

The California Secretary of State is the only authority in California that issues apostilles. Their role is established by California Government Code Section 8205. They verify the signature of the California public official who signed your document. This could be a notary public, a county clerk, or a state registrar.

## How long does it take to get an apostille by mail in California?

Mailing your documents to the California Secretary of State is a common and convenient option for many. However, it is not the fastest. The typical apostille turnaround time California for mailed requests is **5 to 10 business days**. This estimate does not include the time your documents spend in transit to or from their office.

When you mail your documents, you usually send them to the Sacramento office. Make sure to include all required items: the document itself, a cover letter specifying the country where the apostille will be used, and the payment. The California Secretary of State charges $20 per document. This fee can be paid by check or money order.

For example, if you live in Natomas or South Sacramento, mailing might be easier than driving to the office. However, you must factor in the mail delivery time both ways. This could add another 2 to 4 days to the total process. Always use a trackable shipping method. This gives you peace of mind and allows you to monitor your document's journey.
