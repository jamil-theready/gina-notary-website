## What is an Apostille and Why Do I Need One for Colombia?

An apostille is a special certificate. It authenticates the signatures and seals on public documents. It makes them valid for international use. The Hague Convention of 1961 established this simplified process. Colombia is a member of this convention. This means documents originating in California and intended for Colombia require an apostille.

Without an apostille, your California document holds no legal weight in Colombia. This includes everything from a birth certificate to a power of attorney. It serves as an international stamp of approval. It confirms that your document is legitimate for legal use abroad.
